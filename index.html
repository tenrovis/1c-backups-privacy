1C Backups

1C Backups is a private backup utility used to securely transfer encrypted database backups to Google Drive.

Privacy Policy

This application is intended for private use by its authorized operator.

It uses Google Drive access solely to upload, verify, and manage encrypted backup files.

Backup files are encrypted before upload. The application does not sell personal information or share Google user data with third parties.

Google Drive access can be revoked through Google Account security settings.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the application operator through the support email shown on the Google consent screen.
